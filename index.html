About Us

Advanced Technology & Engineering is a Turkish engineering company specializing in inertial navigation systems, MEMS sensor integration, embedded software, avionics and autonomous technologies for defense and aerospace applications. Our expertise spans sensor fusion, GNSS-denied navigation, flight control systems and real-time embedded software development. With proven experience in defense industry projects and TÜBİTAK-funded R&D programs, we develop innovative, high-performance solutions for next-generation autonomous platforms.
